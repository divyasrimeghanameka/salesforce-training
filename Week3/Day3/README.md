# Day 3 - Agentforce and Enterprise AI Systems

## Agentforce Overview

**Agentforce** is Salesforce's AI platform that enables organizations to build intelligent AI agents capable of understanding user requests, retrieving enterprise data, making decisions, and performing actions automatically.

Agentforce integrates:

- AI Models
- Enterprise Data
- Flows
- Apex Logic
- Automation
- Business Processes

### Benefits

- Faster decision-making
- Reduced manual effort
- Increased productivity
- Enhanced user experience
- Intelligent business automation

# AI Workflow

## Enterprise AI Processing Flow

```text
User Submits a Request
          │
          ▼
AI Agent Receives the Request
          │
          ▼
Analyze User Intent
          │
          ▼
Trigger Flow or Apex Logic
          │
          ▼
Retrieve Enterprise Data
          │
          ▼
Execute Business Logic
          │
          ▼
Generate Response
          │
          ▼
Perform Required Action
          │
          ▼
Return Result to User
```

---

## Example Scenario

**Student asks:**

> "How many attendance classes have I missed?"

### Workflow

1. AI Agent understands the request.
2. Attendance records are retrieved.
3. Attendance percentage is calculated.
4. A response is generated.
5. The result is delivered to the student.

# Risks of Enterprise AI Systems

## 1. Hallucinations

**Problem**

AI may generate incorrect or fabricated information.

**Example**

Incorrect attendance calculations.

**Risk**

Poor business decisions.

---

## 2. Incorrect Automation

**Problem**

AI may perform unintended actions.

**Example**

Approving invalid requests.

**Risk**

Business process failures.

---

## 3. Privacy Risks

**Problem**

Sensitive information may be exposed.

**Example**

Unauthorized access to student records.

**Risk**

Security and compliance issues.

---

## 4. Bias

**Problem**

AI recommendations may be unfair.

**Example**

Favoring certain candidates during recruitment.

**Risk**

Unfair business outcomes.

---

## 5. Incorrect Approvals

**Problem**

AI may approve requests without sufficient validation.

**Example**

Approving an ineligible scholarship application.

**Risk**

Governance and compliance issues.

---

## 6. Over-Automation

**Problem**

Too many business decisions are handled automatically.

**Risk**

Reduced human oversight and accountability.

---

# Why Enterprises Need AI Guardrails

AI systems require governance because:

- AI can make mistakes.
- Sensitive information must remain protected.
- Business rules must always be enforced.
- Compliance requirements must be satisfied.
- Critical decisions require human review.

### Benefits of AI Guardrails

- Improved accuracy
- Stronger security
- Greater reliability
- Responsible AI adoption
